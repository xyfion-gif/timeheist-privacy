Privacy Policy for TimeHeist

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Xyfion Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you play our mobile game TimeHeist.

1. Information We Collect

TimeHeist is designed to be fully enjoyable without collecting personal identifying information. Depending on how you interact with the game:

  • Anonymous Profile & Guest Names: We generate a random guest display name (e.g., Guest #42) and anonymous user ID using Google Firebase to store your in-game level progress, unlocked skins, and community map creations.
  • Optional Social Login: If you choose to log in with Google, we only receive your public display name and unique authentication ID to sync your progress across devices. We do not access contacts, private files, or personal feeds.
  • User-Generated Content: When you design and upload a custom puzzle level in the Level Editor, the tile layout and map name are uploaded to our Cloud Firestore database for other players to play and enjoy.

2. Advertising and Purchases

TimeHeist contains NO advertisements and NO real-money in-app purchases. We do not track you across third-party apps or websites for targeted advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Authentication): For saving game data, high scores, and community levels. Google's privacy policy can be viewed at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Children's Privacy

TimeHeist does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can clear your local game data at any time by clearing the application storage in your device settings. To request deletion of community-submitted maps or cloud profile data, please contact us.
